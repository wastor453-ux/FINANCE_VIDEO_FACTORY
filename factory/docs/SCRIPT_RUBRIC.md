# Script rubric (approved by the owner 2026-11-01)

Pass: 85 of 100, no P0 finding, and no zero in Accuracy. The automatic gate (`ffx script-score`) measures part of this; a second Claude (not the writer) scores the rest by reading the script aloud.

| Area | Points | Checked by |
|---|---|---|
| Hook: promise or tension in 10-15 s, type recorded, differs from the last two videos | 15 | gate + reviewer |
| Structure and retention: re-hook near the middle, a new element every 60-90 s, promises paid off | 15 | gate |
| Spoken American style: contractions, breath-length sentences, varied rhythm, no AI tells | 15 | gate + read aloud |
| Visual readiness: beats of 4-15 s, each with a visual idea from our modes, narration does not describe the screen | 20 | gate + reviewer |
| Accuracy and sources: primary sources, dataYear, exact-line quote, claim not stronger than source | 20 | fact-check + gate + reviewer |
| Originality: own structure and examples, no creator named, no video cited | 10 | gate |
| Compliance and safety: disclaimer, no promised returns, legal statements supported | 5 | gate + reviewer |
